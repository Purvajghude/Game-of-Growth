# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences. Primary: local-business owners and brand managers (cafés, gyms, clinics, D2C founders) who found Game of Growth through Instagram or word of mouth; they are deciding whether this 4-person crew can make THEIR brand look this good. Secondary: the four team members themselves, who use the private dashboard (behind login at /login) to win and manage clients.

## Product Purpose

The website IS the portfolio. Game of Growth manages social media, creates content, runs on-location shoots and product demos, builds websites, and ships custom software (CRM, billing, inventory) and apps for local businesses. The site proves capability section by section, then converts visitors into contact-form leads.

## Brand Personality

Premium, editorial, confident. The committed visual identity is the **v2 "Apple Editorial" system: Fraunces (display, with SOFT/WONK variation axes) + Inter/Inter Tight + JetBrains Mono on warm matte paper (#f5f3ee) with deep warm ink (#14130f), terracotta (#b3552e) and matte gold (#a0814a) accents used sparingly, and dark night (#0c0b09) sections for luxury moments.** Numbered mono eyebrows ("07 — The process"), film grain, fine grid backgrounds, a custom dot+ring cursor, and slow quint easing (cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)) are all deliberate, named brand systems. Each landing section is a distinct design world (brutalism, glass, minimal, maximal, SaaS, luxury) held together by this spine.

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION: this system is committed and the user explicitly prefers it. Do not replace it with a dark/neon/cobalt "immersive" look, and do not swap the fonts. Evolve within the system; never restyle it wholesale.**

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-mode sites: near-black voids with a glowing gradient blob, pill glass navs, blue/purple accents. (A previous redesign attempt did exactly this and was rejected as "AI slop.")
- Purple-pink SaaS gradient templates; centered hero over dark mesh with three equal feature cards.
- Anything that abandons the Fraunces editorial voice.

## Design Principles

1. **Identity first.** New sections and pages adopt the v2 editorial tokens and grammar (eyebrows, grain, Fraunces italics) rather than inventing new ones.
2. **Practice what we preach.** Each section demonstrates the service it describes; motion and craft are the portfolio.
3. **Smooth beats loud.** Slow quint easing, restrained reveals, 60fps; effects never stack until the page stutters.
4. **Two registers, one brand.** Public landing is expressive editorial; the dashboard (GoG OS) is calm, light, Notion-like, on the same paper/ink palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses animations. Custom cursor disabled on touch devices and in the dashboard. Keyboard focus visible.
